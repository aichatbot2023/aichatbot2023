## A) Prvi mejl (za nove kontakte)

**Subject:** Patients abroad researching {oblast}: where do they find {Bolnica}?

Dear {Ime},

When a patient in Germany, the UK or the Gulf is told they need {oblast}, the first thing they do today is research it themselves. Usually they end up on forums, ads and search results they can't evaluate.

Kozmodeus Medik gives them a better path. The platform works in 12 languages:

1. **The patient describes the condition in their own words.** A team of AI research agents searches PubMed's 38M+ peer-reviewed papers, ClinicalTrials.gov, openFDA and Europe PMC. Every finding comes back with its source and level of evidence.
2. **They see where in the world it is treated.** A dedicated view shows, country by country, how the condition is being treated and studied, counted directly from ClinicalTrials.gov. For many specialties, Turkey is right there on that map.
3. **They look for a provider in the Kozmodeus Directory.** It lists verified hospitals with their therapies and links to their international patient pages. This is where {Bolnica} should be, with your {oblast} programmes described in your own words.
4. **They contact you prepared.** They have a list of evidence-based questions instead of a screenshot of a forum thread. For your international patient team, that is a better-qualified enquiry from day one.

For your doctors, the same platform is a research tool. The {paket} plan covers your whole team under one subscription: colleagues are added by email and get access immediately. They can run case-specific literature reviews with evidence levels, check trials currently recruiting, and see device recalls and guideline changes in minutes rather than hours.

One principle we don't bend: research results are never ranked by payment. That independence is why patients trust what they read, and why a verified profile next to it carries weight.

We are inviting a first group of leading Turkish hospitals into the Directory. Could I take 15 minutes next week to show you exactly what a patient abroad sees when they research {oblast}, and where {Bolnica} would appear?

Kind regards,

Aleksandar Nikolic
Head of Partnerships · Kozmodeus Medik
